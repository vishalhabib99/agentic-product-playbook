The metrics stack

Every AI product needs all five layers. The North Star says if you are winning; guardrails say if you are winning the wrong way.

What makes it an AI PDLC

  • The eval spec is written in Define, before build. It is the AI equivalent of acceptance criteria and becomes the launch bar.
  • Quality is a distribution, so every phase gate is a threshold on measured rates, not a checklist.
  • Model cost and latency enter at Roadmap, because they change which bets pay off.
  • Trust and risk show up three times: an early screen in Discovery, guardrail requirements in Define, and a red-team sign-off before Launch.
  • Production feedback flows back into the golden set, so every new model version is re-scored on the cases real users hit.
